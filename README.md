# macro-pad
Its a macro pad :D
This macro pad has 18 switches for all your macro needs!
The switches are arranged in a 6 rows 3 columns format.
The pad also sports 2 rotary encoders and a full color TFT screen
# programs used
I used Kicad and Onshape for the hardware parts
# how it's made
I started by laying out my switches and diodes in Kicad until I had an amount I thought was good.
Then I added a Pico as my brain and wired the switches and diodes together and to the Pico.
Next I thought my additional pieces, a screen?, rotary encoders, audio, other switches?
I decided to add a color TFT screen and two rotary encoders.
After wiring those in I went to the PCB editor and updated it to the scematic.
I started to separate the pieces and physically lay them out to the PCB and remembered I forgot to assign the rotary encoders and the switches.
I had to go back and do that and got it done easy. 
From that hiccup it was smooth sailing in Kicad, I was able to layout the proper components and draw the boarders to the PCB.
Then I layed out all the copper traces and ran the DRC and only missed one connection, fixed that and the PCB is done.
